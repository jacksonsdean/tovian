Forming Questions

Yes/No

Add lolhan at the start of a yes/no question.

  • lhan wa ofa fethr?
    English: Do you speak?

Information (wh- style)

Combine a case prefix with lolhan to ask for a specific role:

FormMeaning
ilhanwho
yulhanwhom
lolhanwhat
tulhanwhen
tilhanwhere
silhanhow

Examples:

  • si-lolhan ta fe fethr? — English: How did he speak?
  • ti-lolhan ta fa fethr? — English: Where does he speak?
  • mi-lolhan wa ofa fethr? — English: To whom are you speaking?
